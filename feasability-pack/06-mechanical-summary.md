# Mechanical Summary

The full mechanical specification is in the *Engineering Specification* PDF (Rev B), Part VI. This summary tells the bidder what the enclosure must do and how.

## NULLWEAR/P (personal-issue, body-worn)

| Property | Specification |
|---|---|
| External dimensions | 50 × 35 × 11 mm |
| Mass | 28 g ± 2 g |
| Material | PC-ABS blend, UL94 V-0 flame-rating, matte black with soft-touch coating |
| Process | Two-piece injection-moulded enclosure; ultrasonic weld between top and bottom; internal void potted with polyurethane after PCB+battery integration |
| Ingress protection | IP67 (1 m water immersion for 30 min) |
| Drop resistance | 1.5 m onto concrete on each of 6 faces; no functional or cosmetic damage |
| Operating temperature | -10 to +55 °C |
| Storage temperature | -20 to +60 °C |
| Salt-fog | MIL-STD-810G Method 509.6, 48 h |
| EMC emissions | AS/NZS CISPR 32 Class B |
| Mounting | Two M3 brass inserts (heat-staked) on rear face for belt clip / MOLLE / lanyard |
| LED light pipe | 3 mm acrylic rod, 12 mm length, light-piped from 0805 RGB LED to enclosure top surface |
| USB-C cutout | Right-angle SMD receptacle, sealed via internal EPDM gasket |
| Switch cutover | Single tact switch, recessed 0.5 mm under enclosure surface (button cover for water resistance) |

## Tooling responsibility

The Manufacturer designs and tools all enclosures. CAD files (STEP / STL) become Agency property at acceptance.

The reference Engineering Specification PDF contains dimensioned drawings sufficient to begin tooling. Final CAD details (exact fillets, draft angles, parting lines, ejector pin positions) are at the Manufacturer's discretion provided the external specification is met.

## Cosmetic acceptance

Surface finish: matte black, soft-touch coating, no visible parting line on the top face, no flash > 0.1 mm anywhere, no sink marks visible from > 30 cm.

Branding: serial number laser-etched on rear face; agency logo (if requested) silk-screened or laser-etched on top face. **No vendor logos** unless agreed in the SOW.
